PrintShop flow after advisor feedback

1. Customer selects print details and uploads work file(s).
2. Customer submits the order for staff review. No payment/slip is accepted at this stage.
3. Staff opens the uploaded file(s), verifies paper size, color, sides, actual pages, quantity and unit price.
4. Staff can correct the details and final price, then approve for payment; or reject with a reason.
5. Only after approval (status: waiting_payment) can the customer select payment method and upload a slip.
6. Staff verifies payment. Only paid orders can enter processing/printing.

If an old payment form still appears on the NEW ORDER page:
- stop any old php artisan serve window using port 8000
- run START_PRINTSHOP.bat from this folder, or run: php artisan optimize:clear
- refresh the browser with Ctrl+F5
